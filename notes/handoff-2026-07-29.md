## What was decided

**Open the generative phase (s7):** from-scratch **conditional point diffusion** for completion —
completion recast as *sampling from an explicit learned shape prior* — keeping the controlled
A/C(/B) arms and honest reporting. Hybrid spine: diffusion build in the foreground, **curve
widening/extension as background data-gen** (feeds curve *and* diffusion). Evaluation by the
**two-row protocol**: one-sample CD (honesty row, s6-comparable) + best-of-10 min-CD and TMD
(distribution rows). Phasing: **(1)** unconditional airplane DDPM, gated memorize-1 → memorize-10 →
recognizable samples; **(2)** A-diff, conditioned on the partial, same top-rung data + fixed test
set as s6; **(3)** C-diff → **the diffusion A→C delta = the headline**; B-diff after. Rationale and
tradeoffs: ADR-0009.

## Decoding the advisor's fragments (for the meeting)

- **"Priors"** = what the model believes before seeing the input. Completion *requires* one (the
  back of the chair is not in the scan). Our results in this language: mean-copy is a trivial color
  prior that our learned head hasn't beaten; the generative turn makes the shape prior explicit and
  sampleable.
- **"Treat like diffusion"** = today's machinery for explicit priors, and it targets our observed
  failure mode: regression averages over the many valid completions (over-smooth, mean-ish);
  generation commits to one sharp answer.
- **"Continuous… fix a grid??"** = the representation fork for 3D diffusion: fixed-N points (PVD/PDR)
  vs voxel grid vs latent. We chose points (per-point color is our research object; s6
  comparability). Soundbite: **PVD's answer is "both" — points as the data, a coarse grid *inside*
  the network** (Point-Voxel CNN).
