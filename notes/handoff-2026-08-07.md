## Pelin's method, one paragraph (full read: both notebooks, cell by cell)

Frozen pretrained PoinTr (her fork) completes geometry; a small PointNet part-segments; the new
piece is a **DDPM over the per-point RGB field**, trained *unconditionally* on complete colored
clouds and conditioned only at **sampling time** via RePaint (known-color re-injection at the right
noise level + time-travel resampling). "Part-based" = part-pooled denoiser features (a learned
part-mean), anchorless-part accounting (a fully occluded part draws from the prior, not a
neighbor's color), adaptive resampling budget. Eval = ΔE(Lab) on the missing region vs NN-copy /
part-mean / part-blind ablation / oracle-seg / oracle ceiling; E2 = a fully occluded part.
Her numbers: synthetic stage **works** (22.5 vs ~36, 9/10 wins; E2 crushes baselines); real-texture
first pass **loses** (48.8 vs ~24.5) — but at 4 models × 1 seed with the segmenter never predicting
`engine`. Explicitly a first pass; the scaled honest rerun is this week's experiment.
